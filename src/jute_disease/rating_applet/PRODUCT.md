# Rating instrument context

An owner rates a blind sitting in sheet order, using a keyboard or buttons while inspecting the image. The surface should make each independent choice, its durable save state, and the next action clear without cueing a judgment. It must remain usable during a long sitting, show no identities or rating distributions, and work entirely locally. A failed or unreadable image blocks advance. The neutral gray image surround, equal-weight choices, stable layout and restrained feedback are deliberate study constraints.
